Government & Regulatory Links

Direct links to the Ugandan government agencies and regulatory bodies you'll interact with as a founder. Bookmark these — you'll need them.

🏛️
URSB

Uganda Registration Services Bureau — company registration, business names, trademarks.

 💰
URA

Uganda Revenue Authority — TIN registration, tax filing, customs, and e-tax portal.

 ✅
UNBS

Uganda National Bureau of Standards — product certification, quality marks, import standards.

 💻
NITA-U

National IT Authority — IT governance, data protection registration, digital services compliance.

 📋
KCCA

Kampala Capital City Authority — trading licenses, permits, and city business regulations.

 🏦
Bank of Uganda

Central bank — fintech licensing, forex regulations, financial services guidelines.

 🌱
UIA

Uganda Investment Authority — investment licenses, one-stop centre, incentives for startups.

 🔒
Data Protection Office

Personal Data Protection Office — data controller registration, privacy compliance.
